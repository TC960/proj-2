Data Visualization Gallery

Propositions

For: North America's elderly dependency ratio has reached unsustainable crisis levels compared to historical trends.
Counter: North America's elderly dependency ratio has NOT reached unsustainable crisis levels and is manageable

Visualization Preference

I am leaning toward the counter-proposition visualizations (Images 3 & 4) as they provide more complete context while using fewer deceptive techniques.

Deceptive Techniques Used

Pro-Proposition Visualizations (Images 1 & 2)

  • Alarmist title with emotionally charged language ("CRISIS")
  • Strategic color choices (red for 2020 data to signal danger)
  • Truncated y-axis beginning at ~15% instead of zero
  • Cherry-picked comparison years to maximize contrast
  • Narrow regional focus that excludes global context

Counter-Proposition Visualizations (Images 3 & 4)

  • Stacked area/bar charts that visually minimize elderly dependency proportion
  • Youth dependency positioned to dominate visual space
  • Neutral title obscuring elderly dependency concern
  • Prominent black trend line directing attention to overall decrease
  • Full 60-year timeframe diluting recent acceleration
